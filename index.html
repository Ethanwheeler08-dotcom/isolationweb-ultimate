The shift

Your customers stopped searching. They started asking. Millions of buying decisions now begin with a question to an assistant, and it gives a recommendation, not a results page.

One answer, not ten links

Ten blue links had room for everyone. An answer has room for one name, maybe two. If you're not in it, you're not in the running.

Evidence over volume

Engines cite proof: original data, real reviews, credible mentions. One page a model trusts beats fifty pages of filler.

Presence you own, not clicks you rent

Answers you win keep producing customers after the work that won them is done. The return climbs the longer you stay.
